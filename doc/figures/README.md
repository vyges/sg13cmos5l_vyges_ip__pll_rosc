# PLL presentation figures

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Slide figures for `pll_rosc`, drawn with cirfig_gen (one SVG per figure, sized in pt; insert at
100 % and never resize). Each figure has its SVG, the editable JSON layout, and a PNG preview.

**These figures are drawn from descriptions. The xschem schematics in `xschem/` are the source of
truth.** The xschem exports in `doc/schematics/` are unchanged. Checked against the schematics at
commit `bfc61e8` (which added the `pfd` antenna cells).

## Drawing conventions

- The VDD symbol is `vdd`; the GND symbol is `vss`. Bulk terminals are not drawn (PMOS bulk vdd, NMOS bulk vss throughout).
- Terminal labels name schematic nets: REF=ref, DIV=div, UP=up, DN=dn, PORB=porb, VCTRL=vctrl, IBIAS=ibias,
  IN/OUT = the stage's in/out, VCOOUT=vco_out. The free labels `rstb`, `div` and `n1` name internal nets.
- The DFF symbol's R pin is the cell's active-low RESET_B. The cells' Q_N outputs (nets upn, dnn) connect
  to nothing in the schematic and are not drawn.
- Since `bfc61e8`, the PFD's D inputs are driven by a tie-high cell `Xtiehi` (node `dhi`), not the
  vdd rail. The flops still see a constant 1, and the figures show D tied high. The cell is in
  `doc/schematics/pfd.svg`.
- The folded ring follows the placement described in `doc/implementation.md` (X1-X4 one way, X5-X7 back); electrically it is the plain ring n1 -> X1 -> ... -> X7 -> n1.

## Simplifications

- `pll_top.svg`: the pump's `Mpsrc` and `Mnsnk` are drawn as current sources (they are the mirror outputs;
  the mirror `Mn0`/`Mn1`/`Mp0` and the ibias input are in `pll_cp.svg`); `Mpi`+`Mni` are drawn as one inverter.
  **`Xdiv` is drawn with the palette Buffer symbol as a stand-in**, labelled "1/N, N=2,4,8,16": the palette has
  no divider block, and the divider (four ripple DFFs with Q_N fed back to D, plus a mux4 selecting the tap)
  cannot be drawn at cell level without a crossing per stage. Its rstb, nsel0 and nsel1 inputs are not drawn.
  The VCO uses the palette Ring Oscillator icon, which has no leads: vctrl ends on the ring's left point and
  vco_out leaves its bottom point, so both wires cross the icon's tinted margin.
- `pll_top.svg` has two wire crossings (no dots), both in the PFD reset loop: once the PFD outputs continue
  into the pump, `rstb` cannot reach both RESET_B pins without crossing the dn line once and the lower NAND
  input tap once. `pll_pfd.svg` on its own has no crossings.
- Not drawn: the antenna-protection cells `Xantref` (on ref) and `Xantporb` (on porb) in `pfd`, and the
  six matched dummy inverters `Xdum2`-`Xdum7` (inv_1 on n2-n7, outputs open) in `rosc_vco`.

## Verification

Each table below was generated by extracting connectivity from the `.sch` geometry (instance pin
positions from the PDK/local symbols, nets from the xschem wire labels; hierarchical figures are checked
against the top schematic flattened one level) and comparing it, pin by pin, with the connectivity of
the figure's own wires. The same extractor reproduces the LDO's xschem netlists exactly. A figure passes
only when every drawn pin lands on the schematic net, no figure net joins two schematic nets, no
schematic net is split (except supplies), and every schematic device is either drawn or listed as not
drawn. A drawn symbol that stands for several devices is checked on its ports, as a pseudo-device:
`Xcp/Mpi+Mni` (A=up, Y=upb), `Xvco` (vctrl in, vco_out out), `Xdiv` (clk in, div_out out).

Render check (`tools/render.py`): every figure reports "all wire ends on pins or wires".
